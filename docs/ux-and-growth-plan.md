# UX and growth plan (8 October 2026)

Based on walking the running app as a doctor, a receptionist and a patient on a phone, plus what Indian clinic-software sources say doctors want. The sources are mostly vendor pages, not doctor-written posts, so the "additions" are hypotheses to check with 5-10 real doctors.

## Part A: make what exists easier to use
| # | Problem seen | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | The patient in the room sits below an empty "Needs your attention" card; four equal buttons; greeting always says "afternoon" | Pin "Now seeing" at the top with one primary action; collapse empty attention; real time-of-day greeting |
| A2 | Writing a visit is split over Visit / Documents & billing / Care plan tabs | Prescription and certificate drafting appear on the Visit tab, under the note |
| A3 | Visit tab: empty "Check-in responses" panel and long text before the dictation box | Hide the panel when empty; shorter copy; Dictate is the main button |
| A4 | Assistants page shows three disabled "Admin access needed" buttons to the clinic's own doctor | Say who the admin is and how to get access (permission model unchanged) |
| A5 | Receptionist "Ask your clinic" input squeezed into a tiny box | Layout fix |
| A6 | "Help" opens the safety report; arrow-only button on check-in step 1; English-only staff screens | Real Help page; labelled button; Hindi for the front-desk and doctor "Today" screens as a first pass |

## Part B: what we add
Chosen because each shortens a doctor's day, none needs a vendor, and none changes the safety rules.
| # | Feature | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Visit templates ("fever", "BP follow-up"), "repeat last prescription", favourite medicines, and a note draft that autosaves | Doctors drop software that is slower than paper; this targets the 3-5 minute visit |
| B2 | Patient import from Excel/CSV, with preview, duplicate detection and consent rules | Switching from paper, Excel or another app is the biggest barrier to adoption |
| B3 | "I'm away" mode: leave dates block slots, the public page and WhatsApp assistant say so, affected appointments are listed for doctor-approved notification and rebooking | Every clinic has leave days; reuses the running-late machinery |
| B4 | Share and grow kit: booking link and QR, WhatsApp click-to-chat link, ready captions for Instagram/Google/Facebook, and "where patients came from" tracking | What doctors do on social media is attract patients; this connects it to bookings |
| B5 | Installable app on the phone with a safe offline notice | Many clinics have weak internet |

Not now, with reasons: AI-written health tips and leaflets (needs sourcing and an approval workflow, and the AI key); teleconsultation (needs a video vendor); automatic filling of cancelled slots (already exists as the waiting list).
